I launched Konvo on LinkedIn on September 1st, and that one post reached around 200,000 impressions in the first three days. I didn’t expect that much traction from a problem I was solving for myself.

After that, I started posting trial Reels, but I’d just started school so I was mainly posting low-effort videos. Most of the traction I’d got so far was from that one LinkedIn post. I felt like I was plateauing, doubting myself, is this really it?

My parents told me to make a video that was simple and straight to the point. Then on September 28, I posted a trial Reel explaining my age and my app. It got 2 million views in less than 24 hours.
