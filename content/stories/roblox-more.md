I wanted a pumpkin accessory myself. I liked the look of the expensive Roblox Limiteds, so I started working on my own accessories before Halloween got busy.

People found the items in the Roblox store and joined my Discord. I’d put designs in a poll, see which one people liked, and get that one made. They were telling me what they wanted.

I remember putting an item up for 399 Robux and being surprised that people bought it. After school, I’d come home, talk to the people making the items, check their updates, and see how sales were going.
