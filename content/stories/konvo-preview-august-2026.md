On August 2nd, I submitted Konvo to the App Store. The Apple reviewers kept getting stuck at Instagram’s login verification and asking me for a code. I’d respond, then have to wait another couple of days for the next review.

On August 21, Konvo was accepted after 73 builds and four back-to-back rejections. I found out while having lunch with my parents at Din Tai Fung. I pushed a few more updates before the September launch to make sure everything worked.
