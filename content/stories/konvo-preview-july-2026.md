I originally thought Konvo would be too hard to build, so I never took action. But on July 19, I told my best friend Alisa Du about the idea while we were hanging out in Hong Kong, and she told me I should try. That was the push I needed.

That night, I recorded a huge brain dump in my Notes app, fed it into Claude Code, and started building. By July 26, I had an ugly but working version on my phone, and I sent Alisa the TestFlight version the next day.
