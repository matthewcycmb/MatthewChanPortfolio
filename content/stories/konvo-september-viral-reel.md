I’d just started school, so I was mainly posting low-effort Reels. Most of the traction I’d got so far was from that one LinkedIn post, and I felt like I was plateauing. I was doubting myself, like, is this really it?

My parents told me to make a video that was simple and straight to the point. Then at 7 pm on September 28, I posted a [trial Reel explaining my age and my app](https://www.instagram.com/p/Dd0TQrPo5mZ/). It got 2 million views in less than 24 hours. I used InstantDM so when people commented “app”, they’d get sent the download link.
