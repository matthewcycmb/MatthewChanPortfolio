I posted trial Reels for two weeks straight and nothing was really taking off. Then one hit 2 million views, and another hit 600,000. It was all organic UGC, with $0 in ad spend.

I think the main thing I learned was that copying viral formats that already work actually helps lol. A clear hook, a product demo, and a clear CTA. I used ManyChat to send people the download link when they commented.

You need something that makes someone go wow. For me, that was talking about my age and the fact that I built the app. It could also be a trend or a statement that makes people stop scrolling. Then actually show the product and make it clear how to get it.

By October 8, Konvo had reached 4 million organic views. I just didn't give up.
