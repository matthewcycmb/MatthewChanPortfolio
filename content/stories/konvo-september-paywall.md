I got my first paying users that month, but people were getting stuck on “Loading your plans.” I didn’t realise how urgent it was after the first report. When around ten people reported the same problem, I gave their messages and screenshots to Codex to help figure out what was wrong.

Codex found that the paywall was waiting for three products, including a lifetime purchase Apple hadn’t approved. My local StoreKit test file included it, which hid the problem on my phone. With Codex’s help, I changed build 82 to wait only for products I was actually selling and requested an expedited review that day. I contacted everyone who’d reported the problem, and most confirmed it worked.

Seeing people actually pay for Konvo made me want to keep working on it instead of moving on to another project.
