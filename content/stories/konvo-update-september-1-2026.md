On launch day, around ten users reported that the paywall was stuck. My local StoreKit setup included a lifetime purchase Apple hadn’t approved, so I hadn’t seen the issue on my phone. With Codex’s help, I had a fix in roughly ten minutes and requested expedited App Store review. After release, I contacted the people who’d reported the problem, and most confirmed it worked. Seeing strangers pay for Konvo made me want to keep building it.
