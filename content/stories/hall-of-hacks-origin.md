Hall of Hacks was the first software product I actually built and launched to the public. I’d already won or placed in four hackathons, so I kinda wanted to use that experience and try launching something of my own.

It all started on a walk home from Lougheed Mall. I was thinking about ideas and told my mom, “What if I made a database of winning hackathon projects so people could look through it and get inspired by what wins?”

The idea literally came out of nowhere, but it felt like a good fit for me. People wanted to win hackathons, and I thought I could build it really quickly since I just needed to collect the winning projects from Devpost.

So I went home immediately, opened my Notes app, and literally yapped to my phone for two minutes straight about exactly how, why, and what Hall of Hacks would be.

Claude Fable had just been released that June. I used it for three days straight and luckily finished the website before it got [taken offline after US government restrictions](https://www.anthropic.com/news/redeploying-fable-5) lol.
