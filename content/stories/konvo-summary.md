For the past two years, I’ve tried so many screen time apps to quit doomscrolling, but none of them worked for me. They’d give me a limit, but I could just change it.

So on July 1st, 2026, I decided to delete Instagram for two weeks to see what would happen and whether it would help me stop scrolling.

Fast forward two weeks: when I redownloaded Instagram, I no longer felt the urge to scroll. BUT… I was shocked to see 15+ unread messages from my friends. I’d basically left them all on delivered.

I love my friends, but this was bad… Although deleting Instagram had stopped my doomscrolling, it also made it look like I was ignoring my friends’ messages.

So from that point on, I realised I couldn’t just delete Instagram again because all my friends and messages were there.

But on the other hand, I wanted to delete it again so I wouldn’t have Feed, Explore, and Reels to distract me.

So I thought: what if I made something where Instagram would open straight to my messages, with no Feed, Reels, or Explore to distract me?

That’s why I built Konvo and it’s the app I always wanted on my phone.

I’ve kept month by month notes from the first prototype to launching Konvo and getting real users.
