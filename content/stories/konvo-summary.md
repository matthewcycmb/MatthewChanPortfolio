I’d spent two years trying screen-time apps. When I deleted Instagram for two weeks, my urge to scroll disappeared, but I came back to 15+ unread messages from friends asking to hang out or call. At my school, friends exchange Instagram handles. I wanted to keep those conversations without getting pulled into Reels or the feed every time I replied. That’s why I built Konvo.

Before launch, friends who saw my demo immediately searched for Konvo in the App Store. I had to explain it wasn’t available yet and give them the TestFlight version. Most continued using it.
