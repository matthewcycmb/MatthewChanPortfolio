On August 2nd, I submitted Konvo to the App Store. The Apple reviewers kept getting stuck at Instagram’s login verification and asking me for a code. I’d respond, then have to wait another couple of days for the next review.

My friend Aden Choi tried the TestFlight version and told me the chats should feel more like Instagram. I’d deleted Instagram while building Konvo, so I’d stopped noticing how the original app felt. I spent the next day working on swipe animations so opening and closing a chat didn’t just flash a new screen. It felt much smoother afterward.

On August 21, Konvo was accepted to the App Store after 73 builds and four back-to-back rejections. I found out while having lunch with my parents at Din Tai Fung. I pushed a few more updates before the September launch to make sure everything worked.
